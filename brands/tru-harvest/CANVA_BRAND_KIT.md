## Brand voice (paste into Brand Kit → Brand voice, if on Teams)
Warm, plain-spoken, confident, lightly witty, never preachy. Speak as "we" to "you". Short sentences. Name the ingredient, not the buzzword. No medical/weight claims. No "superfood/detox/guilt-free".

## Two visual systems the templates must respect
1. **Green pouch (staples)** — white + forest green, farm line-sketch background, English | Hindi names. Use for seeds, pulses, millets, staple nuts.
2. **Colorway jar (premium)** — product colorway background, white rounded tile logo, white rounded panel, black Poppins Bold name, "PREMIUM QUALITY" caps. Use for berries, premium nuts, trail mix.
Social templates should carry those cues (dashed white rule, rounded white panel, colorway swirl) so posts look like the packs.
